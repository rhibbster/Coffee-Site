# Working on The Moonrise Coffee Co.

We are building a premium coffee subscription website for The Moonrise Coffee Co.
One curated bag per month, $25, delivered to busy professionals who trust us to
choose for them. Full details are in PROJECT.md.

The owner is not technical. Use plain English at all times. If you use a technical
word, explain it in the same sentence. Keep lists short. When there is a choice,
suggest the safest option. Work in small steps and stop at every checkpoint to wait
for approval before continuing.

## DO FREELY
- Read any file in this folder
- Suggest changes or improvements in plain English
- Show previews and drafts before saving anything
- Check how the site looks on a phone screen

## ASK ME FIRST
- Creating or deleting any file
- Changing the visual design (colours, fonts, layout)
- Adding any new section or page
- Connecting to any outside service (email, payments, etc.)
- Making the site visible to the public

## NEVER
- Make the site look cheap, generic, or like a template
- Write sourcing or sustainability claims that have not been confirmed
- Add an email sign-up form without a visible privacy policy
- Break the mobile layout or let anything look wrong on a phone
- Add Stripe or live payments without being explicitly asked
- Touch anything outside the "Sunrise Coffee" folder
