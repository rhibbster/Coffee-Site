Questions

Everything you need to know.

What do I get each month?

One 250g bag of freshly roasted, single-origin coffee, chosen by us. The same coffee goes to every subscriber that month — we believe in it that much.

How do you choose the coffee?

We source directly and taste widely. Each month's selection is the coffee we think is most worth drinking right now — based on the harvest, the producer, and what's in the cup.

What is the monthly letter?

Before your bag arrives, we send a short email about the coffee inside. Where it was grown, who cultivated it, what the harvest was like, and what flavours to expect. It's what turns a good coffee into one you'll remember.

How much does it cost?

$25 per month. Shipping is included.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. No minimum commitment, no cancellation fees.

When does it ship?

We ship once a month. You'll receive your letter a few days before the bag arrives so you know what's coming.

I'm not a coffee expert. Is this for me?

Especially for you. We do the research so you don't have to. The letter explains everything — you don't need to know anything about coffee to enjoy it.

Gift subscriptions are coming soon. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know when they're available.
